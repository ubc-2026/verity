How Verity reads a market

Four steps, in order. The analysis only proposes a purchase. A separate payment service holds the signer and decides.

  1. 1

    Read the settlement rules

    Verity pulls the event and its terms from the public Gemini Exchange endpoint and flattens the rich-text rules. Reading public market data needs no key.

  2. 2

    Score four risks

    Ambiguity, source, liquidity and correlation, each 1 to 3. Liquidity comes from the bid, the ask and the volume. The rest are code rules, with no LLM.

  3. 3

    Buy only what closes a gap

    Free and cached answers come first. If a gap stays open, Verity proposes one paid report, priced in testnet USDC and bound to a hash of the request.

  4. 4

    Reconcile payment against delivery

    Each on-chain payment is matched to the evidence it bought, so the owner can see what was paid for and what arrived.

Spending rules the agent cannot break

A 25 USDC mission budget that counts money already reserved. Passwords, wallet approvals and promo codes are entered by the owner, never by the agent.

0.01 to 0.10Automatic

Once the shared checks pass: the merchant is allowlisted, the quote is valid and the purchase is not a duplicate.

0.11 to 0.50Automatic, if needed

Only for an open gap with no free or cached answer.

0.51 to 2.00Owner approves

Verity explains the purchase by voice, and the owner signs with their wallet.

Over 2.00Rejected

Never paid, whatever the gap.

Questions

Is this trading advice?

No. Verity places no trades. The market price is the crowd’s view, the risk score is about how the contract settles, and the AI forecast is an experimental model estimate.

Is any real money involved?

No. Payments run on testnet USDC. No control on this site can reach a wallet, a signer or a payment endpoint.

Does “Gemini” mean the Google AI model?

On this site, “Gemini” alone means Gemini Exchange, the prediction-market exchange. The AI model is always written “Google Gemini”, and it only gives an optional second opinion. The risk score itself uses no LLM.

Where does the data come from?

Quotes, price history and candles come live from the public Gemini Exchange API, which needs no key. The two Nov 18 markets use the real captured analysis. News comes from publisher feeds, and research from Exa.

Why would I sign in?

Only to save research runs with your account. Everything else works in the demo.

Does the site hold any API keys?

No. AI, news and research calls go through Verity’s Cloudflare Worker, which keeps its keys as server secrets.

      05 / Architecture
      Four layers, each with one job
      The analysis only proposes. A separate payment service holds the signer and decides. That split is the whole safety argument.
      
    
      
      
      Layer
      Name
      Responsibility
      Technology
      
      
      L1
      Brain
      Reads the contract, scores resolution risk, finds gaps, proposes purchases
      Gemini Exchange public API · verity-rules-v1 (code rules, score of record) · optional Google Gemini second opinion with Cloudflare Workers AI as backup (via Verity’s Worker, advisory)
      
      
      L2
      Wallet
      Pays for exactly the requested resource, once
      x402 · EIP-3009 · USDC on Arc Testnet
      
      
      L3
      Safeguard
      Enforces tiers, the 25 USDC budget and human approval
      Policy engine · ElevenLabs voice · wallet signature
      
      
      L4
      Auditor
      Matches each payment to a verified delivery
      QuickNode Streams · reconciler
      
    
      

    
      
      
      Module 1
      Hardened payments
      1. Canonicalize the request
      2. Hash it with keccak-256
      3. Sign an EIP-3009 authorization
      4. Merchant recomputes and compares
      5. Nonce blocks a replay
      payment service (separate from analysis/)
      
      
      Module 2
      Market risk analysis
      1. Read the event and terms link
      2. Inspect the settlement source
      3. Read bid, ask and volume
      4. Score four categories, 1 to 3
      5. List gaps and free checks
      analysis/verity/rules_analyst.py, live.py
      
      
      Module 3
      Voice spending approval
      1. Match the amount to a tier
      2. Check budget and allowlist
      3. Escalate 0.51–2.00 to the owner
      4. Owner signs with the wallet
      5. Deny or time out cleanly
      policy engine · ElevenLabs
      
      
      Module 4
      Reconciliation
      1. Ingest settlement events
      2. Accept the merchant delivery
      3. Verify the content hash
      4. Deduplicate by event and nonce
      5. Flag every discrepancy
      QuickNode Streams · reconciler

      Verification
      —
      
  
      

      



      
  
      
    
      
      
      Exact resource binding
      A payment is valid only for the request whose hash it was signed over.
      
      
      Bounded spending
      Every purchase sits inside a tier, the mission budget and, above 0.50 USDC, a signed approval.
      
      
      Verified delivery
      A merchant receipt is not proof. The content hash has to match.
      
      
      Independent audit trail
      Settlement events and deliveries are reconciled, and gaps are flagged.
